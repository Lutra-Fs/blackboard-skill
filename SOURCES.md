## Evidence scope

Historical live checks used one university's Blackboard deployment and selected linked services.
The results establish observations for those sources.
They do not establish compatibility with every institution, permission level, or integration.
The checked deployment is an evidence source, not a required preset.

These checks record historical outcomes.
They do not report current service health.
Implementation history stays in Git.

## Verification sources

Offline tests check capture completeness, source mappings, acquisition gaps, authentication failures, reviewed changes, and recovery.
Caption fixtures distinguish absent tracks, an absent requested language, and acquisition failure.
Offline success does not prove current live-service compatibility.
